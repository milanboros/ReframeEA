**Enterprise Architect**
- EA is in charge to define long-term vision how the organization can achieve its strategy
- EA defines the capabilities, information, applications, technology and investment needed to achieve the goal on a conceptual level
- Is in charge to define constraints, governance, requirements and guidelines for the organization.

**IT Architect**
- IA is in charge to translate the EA vision into actionable steps using the constraints, requirements and guidelines.
- Lead team to deliver successful projects. 
- Hold contact with the business, PM and stakeholders
- Responsible to create documentation about:
  - Architectural decisions, 
  - Application system context, 
  - Application architecture overview, 
  - Components model 
  - Deployment unit model

**Business Architect**
- Works closely with Enterprise Architect and Solution Architect
- BA is in charge to define long-term vision how the organization can achieve its strategy
- BA is in charge to define the business domains of the organization (HR, Payroll, Manufacturing, Marketing, etc)
- BA is in charge to document the different business processes are going to flow, what capabilities each need to deliver, what inputs and resources it needs 
- BA define governance around these business processes

**Solution Architect**
- Works closely with IT Architect
- Translates the IT Architecture’s component models into executable code
- Translates the IT Architecture’s deployment model into configuration and network setup
